1. Single upload
$ curl -F "file=@./avatar.png" http://localhost:3000/api/files

2. Multiple upload
$ curl -F "files=@./a.png" -F "files=@./b.pdf" http://localhost:3000/api/files/multiple

HTTP 201

3. List images
$ curl http://localhost:3000/api/files/multiple
[{"filename":"81318860-653f-4777-9434-c0d6b4df9feb.png","size":68,"uploadedAt":"2026-10-07T18:47:18.727Z"},{"filename":"b9547420-a0d9-4751-83ce-efc9889131d2.png","size":68,"uploadedAt":"2026-10-07T18:47:18.664Z"}]
